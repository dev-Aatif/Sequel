Sequel Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Welcome to Sequel. Your personal media consumption, ratings, and viewing habits are private. This Privacy Policy details our data practices, zero-tracking commitment, and how we safeguard your information.

Our Zero-Tracking Pledge: Sequel contains no third-party ad networks, no data brokers, and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal history.

1. Information We Collect

We collect solely the minimum data required to deliver reliable multi-device tracking:

  • Account Credentials: Your email address and a bcrypt-hashed password managed securely by Supabase Authentication. We never receive or store your plaintext password.
  • Media Tracking History: The movies and TV episodes you mark as watched, viewing timestamps, custom watchlist entries, numerical ratings (1–10), and personal reviews.
  • Local Database Storage: Your library is cached locally in an encrypted or sandboxed Android Room database so Sequel functions seamlessly offline.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used strictly to power your personal Sequel experience:

  • To synchronize your watchlist, progress, and reviews across your Android devices via cloud sync.
  • To calculate your personal "Up Next" episode queues and watch statistics.
  • To resolve and fetch media metadata (such as show artwork and episode overviews) from The Movie Database (TMDB).

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): We fetch movie and TV metadata via the TMDB API v3. TMDB receives search and lookup queries; we never transmit your user identity, email, or account information to TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Supabase Cloud Backend: We use Supabase (PostgreSQL 15) for secure cloud synchronization. All cloud tables are protected by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring only your authenticated account can read or write your records.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics): We utilize Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application health and capture crash diagnostics, stack traces, and non-fatal error reports. Firebase Analytics collects anonymized, aggregated operational metrics (such as active sessions and screen views) to diagnose performance bottlenecks. No private watch history, personal reviews, or passwords are transmitted to Firebase.

4. Security & Data Protection

We implement defense-in-depth security measures to protect your information:

  • Transport Layer Security: All client-to-cloud communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Cloud database access is restricted at the PostgreSQL kernel level to matching user tokens (`auth.uid() = user_id`).
  • Sandbox Protection: Android system backup (`android:allowBackup="false"`) is disabled to prevent unauthorized local database extraction via ADB.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion (GDPR / CCPA / Google Play Policy)

You have complete ownership over your data:

  • Export Your History: You can export your library anytime in open formats.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account). This triggers an automated deletion that immediately purges your profile, watched history, reviews, and cloud records from Supabase, alongside wiping your local Room database.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled Sequel or cannot access the app, you may request permanent deletion of your account and associated cloud data by emailing privacy@sequel.app or filing a request via our Support Desk. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Sequel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. Significant changes will be accompanied by an in-app notice.
