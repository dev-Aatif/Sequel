Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Sequel! Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with a seamless tracking experience:

  • Account Information: Your email address and a secure password hash when you register an account.
  • App Data: Your watch history, ratings, reviews, and watchlist items, synced securely to the cloud so you can access them anywhere.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to deliver and improve the Sequel experience:

  • To sync your watch history and preferences across devices.
  • To display community reviews and average ratings for movies and TV shows.
  • To provide technical support and respond to your inquiries.

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to ensure your data is safe. We use secure databases (Supabase) with row-level security, meaning only you can access your private data. Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party services like TMDB (The Movie Database) to fetch show and movie metadata. We do not share your personal data with TMDB. We use Supabase for backend authentication and database storage.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can export your data or delete your account at any time directly within the app settings. Deleting your account will permanently erase your watch history, reviews, and user profile from our servers.

Note on Deletion: Ensure you have an active internet connection when initiating account deletion so we can securely remove your data from our cloud servers.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. We will notify you of any significant changes via an in-app notice.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@sequel-app.com.
